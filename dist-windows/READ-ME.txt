Lust: games in Lua, run in a window of their own.

1. Double click lust.exe (or lust-games.bat): a list of the games in the folder "games".
   Click one or use arrows and Enter. F1 in a game comes back to the list.
2. A game of your own: put its folder (with a world.lua inside) into "games".
3. An AI that builds a game for you: double click lust-dev.bat and follow what it says.
   Closing that window only puts it into the taskbar: the AI goes on. It ends when you quit
   Claude Desktop.
   The square sign top right: its ring is the AI (grey: none yet, green: connected, blue: it
   listens to you), its inside your microphone (it grows when you speak).
   To give the AI orders by voice: unpack lust-voice-ru.zip into this folder (the russian
   speech recognizer, about 45 MB, works without internet), then say "listen" (or "слушай")
   in the chat. The words it heard show under the sign. "Stop" (стоп) ends it.
4. If a window does not open on your computer, lust opens the game in your browser.
5. The microphone: a game that asks for it, or the AI of lust-dev, turns it on and the sign
   shows it; Windows may ask once, or block it in Settings, Privacy, Microphone. The sound
   never leaves this computer.

If Windows SmartScreen says "unknown publisher": the program is not signed (a signature costs
money). "More info", then "Run anyway".
